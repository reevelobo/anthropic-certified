## Overview

Introduces the **AI Fluency Framework** — a structured approach to effective, ethical, and safe human-AI collaboration. This is the conceptual foundation for the entire AI Fluency series.

---

## The 4D Competencies

| Competency | Focus |
|-----------|-------|
| **Delegation** | Deciding *what* to hand off to AI and *when* |
| **Description** | Communicating clearly to get the output you need |
| **Discernment** | Evaluating, verifying, and improving AI outputs |
| **Diligence** | Taking responsibility for AI-assisted work |
