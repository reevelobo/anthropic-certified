# A closer look at description 
### Description: Communicating Effectively with AI

The **Description** competency focuses on how we communicate with AI systems. It is a core component of AI fluency because the quality of AI outputs often depends on the quality of the instructions and context we provide.

Description is not just about writing prompts. It is about effectively guiding AI interactions by clearly communicating goals, context, expectations, methods, and desired behaviors.

---

### What Is Description?

Description is the ability to communicate effectively with AI in order to:

- Explain tasks.
- Ask questions.
- Provide context.
- Guide reasoning and problem-solving.
- Correct misunderstandings.
- Shape the overall interaction.

Think of Description as building a bridge between:

- Your intentions
- The AI's capabilities

The clearer the communication, the better the results.

---

### Why Description Matters

AI systems cannot read minds.

If instructions are vague, the AI must make assumptions, which can lead to:

- Incomplete results
- Incorrect outputs
- Unwanted formats
- Misaligned responses

Instead of assuming the AI knows what you want, clearly communicate:

- The task
- The context
- The desired outcome
- Any constraints or requirements

---

### Example

#### Vague Request

> "Make dinner."

#### Detailed Request

> "Prepare a vegetarian pasta dinner for four people using ingredients commonly found at home. The meal should take less than 30 minutes and include a simple side dish."

The second description provides significantly more information, making it easier to achieve the desired result.

The same principle applies when working with AI.

---

### The Three Components of Description

The Description competency consists of three key elements:

1. Product Description
2. Process Description
3. Performance Description

Together, they create a framework for effective AI communication.

---

### 1. Product Description

#### Definition

**Product Description** is the ability to clearly define what you want the AI to create or provide.

It focuses on the final output.

---

### Questions to Consider

When describing the desired product, ask:

- What exactly do I want?
- What is the goal?
- What format should the response use?
- Who is the audience?
- What level of detail is needed?
- What tone or style should be used?

---

### Information to Include

#### Context

Provide background information that helps the AI understand the situation.

Examples:

- Business environment
- Educational setting
- Personal goals
- Target audience

---

#### Desired Outcome

Clearly explain what should be produced.

Examples:

- Report
- Email
- Summary
- Lesson plan
- Presentation
- Analysis

---

#### Format Requirements

Specify:

- Length
- Structure
- Layout
- Output format

Examples:

- Markdown
- Bullet points
- Executive summary
- Table
- Blog post

---

#### Audience

Identify who will consume the output.

Examples:

- Students
- Managers
- Customers
- Technical professionals
- General public

---

### Product Description Example

> Create a one-page summary of cloud security best practices for new IT administrators. Use simple language, bullet points, and include practical examples. The audience is early-career infrastructure professionals.

---

### Key Idea

Product Description answers:

> **"What do you want the AI to create?"**

---

### 2. Process Description

#### Definition

**Process Description** is the ability to guide how the AI approaches a task.

It focuses on the method rather than just the final outcome.

---

### Why Process Matters

Sometimes *how* a task is performed is as important as the final result.

Just as you might ask a colleague to follow a particular process, you can guide AI in a similar way.

---

### Ways to Guide the Process

#### General Guidance

Provide broad instructions.

Example:

> Focus on practical recommendations rather than theoretical discussion.

---

#### Step-by-Step Instructions

Define a sequence of actions.

Example:

1. Analyze the problem.
2. Identify root causes.
3. Provide solutions.
4. Rank solutions by impact.

---

#### Examples and Demonstrations

Show the AI what good output looks like.

Example:

> Follow the style of this sample report.

---

### Questions to Consider

- What information should the AI use?
- What order should tasks follow?
- Should specific methods or frameworks be applied?
- Are there constraints or requirements?
- Should the AI focus on certain issues?

---

### Process Description Example

> First summarize the article, then identify key risks, and finally provide three actionable recommendations. Focus on security and compliance impacts.

---

### Key Idea

Process Description answers:

> **"How should the AI approach the task?"**

---

### 3. Performance Description

#### Definition

**Performance Description** is the ability to define how the AI should behave during an interaction.

It focuses on interaction style and communication preferences.

---

### Why Performance Matters

AI is not a database or vending machine.

It is an interactive system whose behavior can be influenced by instructions.

Different situations require different types of AI behavior.

---

### Questions to Consider

#### Exploration vs. Precision

Do you want the AI to:

- Explore many possibilities?
- Help identify a single best answer?

---

#### Challenge vs. Support

Do you want the AI to:

- Challenge assumptions?
- Act as a supportive assistant?

---

#### Detailed vs. Concise

Do you want:

- Comprehensive explanations?
- Brief, direct answers?

---

#### Transparency

Should the AI:

- Explain its reasoning?
- Simply provide results?

---

### Performance Description Examples

#### Critical Thinking Partner

> Challenge my assumptions and identify weaknesses in my plan.

#### Brainstorming Partner

> Generate as many creative possibilities as possible without evaluating them initially.

#### Executive Assistant

> Keep responses concise, action-oriented, and professional.

#### Teacher

> Explain concepts step by step and verify understanding before moving forward.

---

### Key Idea

Performance Description answers:

> **"How should the AI behave while helping me?"**

---

### How the Three Elements Work Together

#### Product Description

Defines:

> What should be created?

#### Process Description

Defines:

> How should it be created?

#### Performance Description

Defines:

> How should the AI interact with me?

When combined, these three elements create much more effective AI interactions.

---

### Example of All Three Together

#### Product

> Create a project plan for migrating workloads to Microsoft Azure.

#### Process

> Analyze requirements first, identify risks second, and then create a phased migration strategy.

#### Performance

> Act as a senior cloud architect. Be direct, practical, and challenge unrealistic assumptions.

This combination gives the AI clear direction across all aspects of the task.

---

### Key Takeaways

- Description is the AI Fluency competency focused on effective communication with AI systems.
- AI outputs improve when goals, context, and expectations are clearly communicated.
- AI cannot read minds and should not be expected to infer important details.
- **Product Description** defines what the AI should create.
- **Process Description** defines how the AI should approach the work.
- **Performance Description** defines how the AI should behave during the interaction.
- Combining all three elements transforms AI from a generic assistant into a more effective and personalized thinking partner.
- Strong Description skills lead to better outcomes, fewer misunderstandings, and more productive human-AI collaboration.
