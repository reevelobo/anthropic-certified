### Self-Directed AI Fluency Activities

**Estimated Time:** Self-Paced

These activities are designed to help you continue developing your AI fluency after completing the **AI Fluency: Framework and Foundations** course.

You can choose the activities that best align with your goals, interests, and professional needs, or use them as inspiration to create your own learning plan.

---

### Activity 1: Build a Personal AI Fluency Plan

Work with an AI assistant to create a structured plan for improving your AI fluency over time.

---

### Step 1: Current Competency Assessment

Evaluate your current proficiency in each of the four AI Fluency competencies.

#### Rate Yourself

For each competency, assign a proficiency level:

- Novice
- Developing
- Confident

Competencies:

- Delegation
- Description
- Discernment
- Diligence

---

#### Strengths and Growth Areas

Document:

- Current strengths
- Areas for improvement
- Skills you would like to strengthen

Questions to consider:

- Which competency feels strongest?
- Which competency feels least developed?

---

#### Assess Your AI Interaction Modes

Evaluate how effectively you currently use:

##### Automation

AI completes tasks according to instructions.

##### Augmentation

You collaborate with AI as a thinking partner.

##### Agency

AI acts independently on your behalf.

Identify:

- Which mode you use most effectively
- Which mode you use least effectively

---

### Step 2: Identify Development Priorities

Choose one or two competencies as your primary development focus.

---

#### Select Priority Competencies

Examples:

- Improve prompting through Description
- Strengthen evaluation skills through Discernment
- Improve ethical decision-making through Diligence

---

#### Define Context-Specific Goals

Consider:

- What would be most valuable in your work?
- What would help in your studies?
- What would improve personal productivity?

---

#### Select AI Interaction Goals

Decide which interaction types you want to develop further:

- Automation
- Augmentation
- Agency

---

### Step 3: Create an Action Plan

Develop practical activities and milestones.

---

#### Practice Activities

Examples:

- Daily prompting exercises
- AI-assisted writing tasks
- Collaborative brainstorming sessions
- Regular output evaluation practice
- Diligence and ethics reviews

---

#### Practice Schedule

Define:

- Frequency
- Duration
- Review checkpoints

Examples:

- 15 minutes daily
- 1 hour weekly
- Monthly competency reviews

---

#### Resources and Tools

Identify:

- AI platforms
- Learning materials
- Communities
- Books
- Courses

---

#### Success Measures

Determine how you will recognize improvement.

Examples:

- Better prompt quality
- Faster task completion
- Improved output quality
- Greater confidence in evaluation
- More effective human-AI collaboration

---

### Recommendation

Use Claude or another AI assistant as a thinking partner throughout the planning process.

Ask for:

- Feedback on self-assessments
- Suggestions for improvement
- Personalized learning activities

---

## Activity 2: Build a Personal Prompt & Pattern Library

Create a personal collection of prompts, templates, and strategies that consistently produce useful results.

---

### Step 1: Create Common Task Templates

Identify recurring AI tasks.

---

#### Examples

- Writing emails
- Creating presentations
- Research summaries
- Meeting notes
- Brainstorming sessions
- Code reviews
- Study guides
- Project planning

Aim for:

- 5 to 10 recurring tasks

---

#### Build Reusable Prompt Templates

For each task:

- Create a template that consistently performs well.
- Include placeholders for variable information.
- Add any useful instructions or constraints.

---

### Template Components

Include:

- Context
- Goals
- Audience
- Format requirements
- Style preferences
- Evaluation criteria

---

### Step 2: Document Effective Strategies

Keep notes about techniques that work particularly well.

---

### Description Strategies

Record:

- Successful context structures
- Useful examples
- Effective role assignments
- Strong process descriptions

---

### Discernment Strategies

Document patterns that help evaluate outputs.

Examples:

- Fact-checking methods
- Quality review checklists
- Bias detection techniques
- Verification workflows

---

### Interaction Mode Strategies

Track which approaches work best for:

#### Automation

Task-focused execution.

#### Augmentation

Collaborative ideation and problem-solving.

#### Agency

Autonomous AI workflows and agents.

---

### Step 3: Build a Personal Reference System

Organize your library for easy access.

---

#### Include

- Prompt templates
- Successful outputs
- Lessons learned
- Best practices
- Refinement notes

---

#### Maintain the Library

Create a process for:

- Updating templates
- Adding new discoveries
- Removing outdated approaches

As AI systems evolve, your library should evolve too.

---

### Recommendation

Ask Claude to analyze your most successful interactions and identify common patterns.

## Activity 3: Game Night!

Games are an enjoyable way to strengthen Description and Discernment skills.

Puzzles are especially useful because they require:

- Precise instructions
- Clear reasoning
- Careful evaluation

These activities provide practical experience in guiding AI thinking and evaluating AI responses.

---

### Swap Riddles

#### How It Works

1. Create a riddle.
2. Have Claude guess the answer.
3. Ask Claude to explain its reasoning.
4. Guide Claude toward the correct answer without revealing it.
5. Reverse roles and solve Claude's riddle.

---

### Example Riddle

> Five-score and more drums I tap to paint a symphony of light. What am I doing?

**Answer:**

> Typing on a computer keyboard (typically around 104 keys) to create pixels on a monitor.

---

### Learning Opportunity

Focus on:

- AI reasoning process
- Context interpretation
- Quality of explanations

---

## Cooperative Crosswords

Solve crossword clues together.

---

### Why This Helps

You'll practice:

- Clarifying assumptions
- Refining interpretations
- Guiding reasoning

Examples:

- "It starts with B."
- "The clue is only five letters."
- "Maybe 'swallow' refers to the bird, not drinking."

---

### Skills Developed

- Description
- Discernment
- Collaborative reasoning

---

## Word Association

Choose 12 to 20 random words and work together to identify relationships and themes.

---

### Focus Areas

Observe how Claude:

- Groups concepts
- Finds patterns
- Builds connections

Challenge weak reasoning and encourage stronger interpretations.

---

### Practice the Three Forms of Discernment

#### Product Discernment

Ask:

- Do the answers make sense?
- Are the themes cohesive?

---

#### Process Discernment

Ask:

- How is Claude reasoning?
- Is it considering alternatives?
- Is it becoming fixated on one interpretation?

---

#### Performance Discernment

Ask:

- Is Claude communicating clearly?
- Does it ask useful questions?
- Does it build on suggestions effectively?

---

## More Game Suggestions

These activities provide additional opportunities to practice Description and Discernment.

---

### Twenty Questions

#### Activity

- Claude thinks of an object, person, or concept.
- You ask yes/no questions to identify it.
- Reverse roles afterward.

#### Skills Practiced

- Question creation
- Information gathering
- Strategic reasoning

---

### Collaborative Storytelling

#### Activity

- Write one sentence.
- Claude adds the next sentence.
- Continue alternating.

#### Skills Practiced

- Context maintenance
- Creative collaboration
- Narrative development

---

### A Wizard Approaches You in the Tavern

#### Activity

Play a role-playing game with Claude acting as:

- Player
- Dungeon Master
- Narrator

#### Skills Practiced

- Improvisation
- Description
- Collaborative storytelling

---

### Concepts & Constraints

#### Activity

Choose:

- A complex idea
- Strict communication constraints

Example:

> Explain machine learning using only cooking metaphors.

Then compare your explanation with Claude's.

#### Skills Practiced

- Precision
- Creativity
- Communication

---

### Advanced Puzzling

#### Activity Options

- Create logic puzzles for Claude.
- Solve puzzles that Claude creates.
- Collaboratively tackle difficult problems.

For programmers:

- Work through challenging mathematical or programming problems.
- Explore projects such as Project Euler.

**Website:** https://projecteuler.net/

---

### Why These Activities Matter

These exercises reinforce the AI Fluency competencies through practice.

They help you:

- Improve delegation decisions.
- Communicate more clearly.
- Evaluate outputs more effectively.
- Develop stronger human-AI collaboration habits.

Most importantly, they help transform AI from a simple tool into a productive thinking partner.

---

### Key Takeaways

- AI fluency improves through consistent practice and experimentation.
- Create a personal development plan focused on the Four Ds:
  - Delegation
  - Description
  - Discernment
  - Diligence
- Build a reusable prompt and strategy library to improve future interactions.
- Games and puzzles provide practical opportunities to strengthen Description and Discernment skills.
- AI fluency is a continuous learning process that evolves alongside AI technology.
- The best results come from actively collaborating with AI, evaluating outcomes, and refining your approach over time.
