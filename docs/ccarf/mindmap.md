# Mind Map

The full CCAR-F syllabus — 5 domains, 30 task statements, every "Knowledge
of" and "Skills in" bullet from the exam guide — as a pannable, zoomable mind
map. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click a node to expand or collapse it.
Branch colors match the domain colors in the [Study Runway](study-runway.md).

<a href="../assets/mindmap.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open full-screen ↗</a> is the more
comfortable way to explore it; the embed below works too.

`docs/ccarf/assets/outline.txt` and see the comment at the top of
`docs/ccarf/assets/rebuild-mindmap.js` for the one-line regenerate command.
