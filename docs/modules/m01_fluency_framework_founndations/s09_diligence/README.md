### Diligence: Ethical and Responsible AI Use

The **Diligence** competency focuses on the ethical, safe, transparent, and responsible use of AI.

While the other AI Fluency competencies largely emphasize effectiveness and efficiency, Diligence focuses on ensuring that AI is used responsibly and that humans remain accountable for the outcomes.

At its core, Diligence is about taking responsibility for your AI interactions, decisions, and outputs.

---

### What Is Diligence?

Diligence is the ability to use AI in a way that is:

- Ethical
- Safe
- Transparent
- Accountable
- Responsible

It encourages users to think beyond simply obtaining results and instead consider the broader impacts of AI-assisted work.

---

### Why Diligence Matters

Using AI responsibly requires asking important questions such as:

- What are the consequences of using this AI system?
- Who might be affected by the output?
- Could errors or inaccuracies cause harm?
- How is data being used and protected?
- Does this use align with ethical standards and organizational policies?

Diligence ensures that AI collaboration considers both immediate outcomes and broader societal implications.

---

### AI Responsibility Analogy

Using AI responsibly is similar to driving a car.

When driving, the goal is not simply to reach a destination efficiently.

Drivers must also:

- Follow rules.
- Protect others.
- Consider safety.
- Act responsibly.

Likewise, AI users must think beyond productivity and consider:

- Risks
- Consequences
- Privacy
- Fairness
- Accountability

---

### The Three Components of Diligence

The Diligence competency consists of:

1. Creation Diligence
2. Transparency Diligence
3. Deployment Diligence

Together, these help ensure responsible AI use throughout the entire AI lifecycle.

---

### 1. Creation Diligence

#### Definition

**Creation Diligence** is the ability to be intentional and critical about:

- Which AI systems you use.
- How you use them.
- What information you provide to them.

---

### Questions to Ask

Before using an AI system, consider:

- How was this system trained?
- What data was used to train it?
- Who owns the data I provide?
- Who might gain access to that data?
- How is my privacy being protected?
- How is sensitive information being handled?
- Does this system align with my values?
- Does it comply with organizational policies?

---

### Privacy and Data Protection

One major focus of Creation Diligence is protecting information.

Before sharing data with AI, evaluate:

- Confidentiality risks
- Data retention policies
- Security protections
- Organizational guidelines

---

### Example

Before uploading confidential company information into an AI assistant:

- Review organizational policies.
- Verify approved usage guidelines.
- Confirm that the AI platform provides appropriate data protection.

---

### Key Idea

Creation Diligence asks:

> **"Should I use this AI system and in this way?"**

---

### 2. Transparency Diligence

#### Definition

**Transparency Diligence** is the ability to be honest and open about AI's involvement in creating work.

It focuses on disclosure and trust.

---

### Why Transparency Matters

Different environments may have different expectations regarding AI disclosure.

Examples include:

- Workplace projects
- Academic assignments
- Research papers
- Creative content
- Business reports

Regardless of context, users have a responsibility to understand and meet transparency expectations.

---

### Questions to Ask

- Who needs to know AI was involved?
- What should be disclosed?
- When should the disclosure occur?
- How detailed should the disclosure be?

---

### Benefits of Transparency

Transparency helps:

- Build trust.
- Maintain credibility.
- Support informed decision-making.
- Promote ethical collaboration.

---

### Example

Suppose AI helps draft a team proposal.

Transparency might involve informing colleagues:

- That AI assisted in drafting.
- Which sections received AI support.
- How AI contributed to the final document.

This allows everyone to understand the role AI played.

---

### Key Idea

Transparency Diligence asks:

> **"Who needs to know that AI contributed to this work?"**

---

### 3. Deployment Diligence

#### Definition

**Deployment Diligence** is the ability to take responsibility for AI-generated outputs before using or sharing them.

This focuses on accountability.

---

### Human Accountability

Even when AI creates content:

- Humans remain responsible.
- AI does not bear accountability.
- Users must verify quality and accuracy.

---

### Verification Responsibilities

Before sharing AI-generated content:

- Fact-check claims.
- Verify sources.
- Detect biases.
- Review correctness.
- Confirm legal usage rights.
- Ensure quality standards are met.

---

### Example

A journalist using AI to draft an article must still:

- Verify all facts.
- Validate sources.
- Review claims.
- Ensure journalistic standards are maintained.

The same standards apply as if the journalist had written the article independently.

---

### Questions to Ask

- Is this information accurate?
- Can I confidently stand behind this output?
- Has it been verified?
- Have biases been addressed?
- Is it appropriate for publication or distribution?

---

### Key Idea

Deployment Diligence asks:

> **"Am I willing to take responsibility for this output?"**

---

### Diligence in Different Contexts

Expectations may vary across environments.

#### Personal Use

Focus may be on:

- Privacy
- Accuracy
- Responsible sharing

---

#### Academic Use

Focus may include:

- Citation requirements
- Disclosure expectations
- Academic integrity

---

#### Professional Use

Focus may include:

- Compliance policies
- Data protection
- Confidentiality
- Risk management

---

### Developing Personal AI Guidelines

Because AI standards continue to evolve, users should establish personal guidelines.

Consider defining:

- What information you will never share with AI.
- When AI disclosure is required.
- How outputs will be validated.
- Which AI tools you trust for particular tasks.

---

### Staying Informed

AI technology continues to evolve rapidly.

Diligence includes staying informed about:

- New capabilities
- Emerging risks
- Organizational policies
- Industry standards
- Legal frameworks
- Regulatory developments

Responsible AI use requires ongoing learning.

---

---

#### Transparency Diligence

Determines:

> Who should know AI was involved?

---

#### Deployment Diligence

---

### The Role of Human Responsibility

Diligence reinforces an important principle:

> AI may assist with the work, but humans remain responsible for the results.

Humans are responsible for:

- Ethical decisions
- Accuracy
- Compliance
- Transparency
- Accountability

---

### Key Takeaways

- Diligence is the AI Fluency competency focused on ethical and safe AI use.
- It encourages responsibility, transparency, and accountability throughout AI interactions.
- **Creation Diligence** evaluates which AI systems to use and how information is shared.
- **Transparency Diligence** focuses on appropriate disclosure of AI involvement.
- **Deployment Diligence** ensures users verify and take responsibility for AI-generated outputs.
- Responsible AI use requires consideration of privacy, security, ethics, and organizational policies.
- Humans remain accountable for all outputs created with AI assistance.
- Effective AI fluency requires not only effectiveness and efficiency but also responsibility and ethical judgment.
