# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Training Peak Week

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete aiming to maximize carbohydrate intake and hydration during marathon training peak week:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (120g), Fat (80g), Carbs (480g)

**Meal Plan:**

* **Breakfast (6:30 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), honey (30g), and whole milk (240g). Side of whole wheat toast (50g) with jam (20g).
  * Protein: 18g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 110g

* **Pre-Workout Snack (8:00 AM):** Energy bar (65g) and sports drink (500ml).
  * Protein: 10g, Fat: 5g, Carbs: 70g

* **Lunch (12:30 PM):** Pasta (100g dry weight) with lean ground turkey (120g) and marinara sauce (150g). Side salad with olive oil dressing (15g oil). Orange juice (250ml).
  * Protein: 35g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 95g

* **Post-Workout Snack (3:30 PM):** Rice cakes (60g) with almond butter (25g) and apple (120g). Sports drink (500ml).
  * Protein: 12g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Dinner (6:30 PM):** Grilled chicken breast (150g), white rice (120g dry weight), steamed carrots (150g), and olive oil (10g). Whole wheat roll (40g).
  * Protein: 35g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 105g

* **Evening Snack (8:30 PM):** Greek yogurt (200g), granola (40g), and berries (80g). Coconut water (200ml).
  * Protein: 18g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 65g

* **Hydration:** Water throughout the day (minimum 3-4 liters) + electrolyte drinks during/after training sessions.

This meal plan prioritizes easily digestible carbohydrates, moderate protein for muscle recovery, and consistent hydration to support endurance performance and glycogen replenishment during peak marathon training.
